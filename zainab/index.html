To Zainab ✧The letter ↗
A letter I should have written sooner

Dear Zainab.

There are things I've wanted to tell you for a very long time. I just never knew how to begin.

Read my heart ↓
01 / THE THINGS UNSAID

I always wanted us back.

I don't know if I ever made that clear. Even after everything that happened between us, a part of me always wanted to reach out, to find our way back to the friendship we had. I just didn't know how to do it without getting the words wrong.

I'm sorry for our fallout. I'm especially sorry for the times I made you feel like I didn't care. I did. More than I knew how to show. I even cried about it when I was alone, because you were one of the people I trusted most with everything. And I still do.

✧ ✦ ✧
02 / UNDER THE SILENCE

My quiet was never indifference.

I'm good with words, but feelings are harder. I've given a lot of myself to people and been hurt in return. Somewhere along the way, I became colder, quieter, sometimes numb. That isn't all I am, but it is a part of me I'm still trying to understand.

I feel that distance with almost everyone, not just you. It wasn't until Raheem that I began to understand something important: a bond doesn't become less real just because you can't always feel a storm of emotion inside you.

None of that excuses the ways I hurt you. I just want you to know that my silence was never proof that you mattered less.

✧ ✦ ✧
03 / THE WAYS I CARED

Even when you couldn't see it.

You have always been a priority to me, whether you knew it or not. I never entertained people speaking badly about you, and I won't. When I warned you about certain people, it came from concern. I believe you learn who someone is by watching how they treat everyone, not only how they treat you.

But I also know your choices are yours. I don't need you to agree with every judgment I make to care about you. I only want you safe, respected, and surrounded by people who mean well.

And I want to learn how to show that care in ways you can actually feel.

✧ ✦ ✧
04 / A LITTLE LIGHT

You remind me of life.

I've told you before that you feel like another shot at life to me. I mean the glimmer, the joy, the innocence, the love. You carry so much of what I remember feeling before I grew tired.

“Would you lend me a little of your light when mine feels dim? Would you show me the world through your eyes when I forget how to see its wonder?”

Not because I want to become you. I admire you far too much to ever want to be a copy. Nobody else could be you. And I don't expect you to carry my pain or make me whole. I just hope we can keep reminding each other that there is still beauty in the world.

✧ ✦ ✧
05 / NO PERFECT PROMISES

I can promise to try.

I won't tell you I'll be the best friend in the world. I won't make grand promises I can't guarantee. There may be days when I'm not emotionally expressive, when I don't know what to say, when I seem distant even though I'm here.

But I can listen. I can learn. I can make the effort to be present, mentally and practically, even when emotions are difficult. And when I get something wrong, I can take responsibility instead of hiding behind what I've been through.

I care about you. I always have. I want to be better at letting you know.

One last thing

One more chapter?

Zainab, I don't want to erase what happened. I want to acknowledge it, learn from it, and ask whether we could start again — slowly, honestly, in whatever way feels right to you.

I still trust you. I still care. And if there's room in your world for me, I'd love to be your friend again.

Would you lend me your hand in friendship, one more time?

You don't have to answer here. Whatever you decide, I will respect it.

WITH CARE, ALWAYS ✧
