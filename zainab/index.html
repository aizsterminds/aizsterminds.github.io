Before you leave this little place…

Happy Birthday, Zainab.

If perfection exists somewhere, I think it has to carry at least a glimpse of you in its reflection.

And I do not mean that because I want to put you on a pedestal, or pretend you are somehow above being human. I mean it because almost every quality I have ever learned to admire seems to exist in you at once — kindness without performance, softness without weakness, playfulness that makes people breathe a little easier, empathy that makes people feel understood, and a kind of unconditional love that never asks to be praised for simply being there.

I see it in the way you are a daughter, a sister, and a friend. You make care look effortless. You make goodness feel ordinary, as if it costs you nothing, even though I know the world does not always make it easy to remain that way. There are days I genuinely wish I could grow into even half the woman you already are.

More than anything, I hope your innocence prevails. Not innocence as in never knowing how difficult life can be — but that radiant part of you that still chooses tenderness after knowing enough to become guarded. I do not want harsh people or harsh experiences to teach you that softness is something you have to lose in order to survive.

Allah has kept you safe in ways only He knows, and InshaAllah that remains true for every year ahead of you. May He keep you far from people who would make you question your worth, far from experiences that would harden what is beautiful in you, and close to people who recognise that your heart is something to protect, not test.

And if I get one selfish wish in all of this, it is that this friendship is always one place where you never have to become less of yourself to feel safe.
